## 📁 Course Details

### 🔹 [CSE 211 — Discrete Mathematics](./CSE211-Discrete-Mathematics/)
* **Key Topics:** Mathematical Reasoning, Proofs, Sets, Relations, Graphs, Trees, Counting Techniques.
* **Contents:**
  * `lesson_notes/` — Lecture and class notes.
  * `midterm/` & `CSE211_MIDTERM.pdf` — Midterm study resources and past questions.
  * `final/` — Final examination study material.
  * Textbooks by *Bernard Kolman* and *Kenneth H. Rosen*.

### 🔹 [CSE 231 — Circuits and Electronics](./CSE231-Circuits-and-Electronics/)
* **Key Topics:** Ohm's Law, Kirchhoff's Laws, Nodal & Mesh Analysis, Thévenin/Norton Equivalents, Operational Amplifiers, Diodes & Transistors.
* **Contents:**
  * `slides/` & `CSE231 Slides/` — Official lecture presentation slides.
  * `homeworks/` & Homework PDFs — Homework assignments and problem sets.
  * `midterm konuları/` & `final/` — Exam study topics and reference files.
  * *Fundamentals of Electric Circuits* (Alexander & Sadiku) textbook and complete solution manuals.

### 🔹 [CSE 233 — Circuits and Electronics Lab](./CSE233-Circuits-and-Electronics-Lab/)
* **Key Topics:** Physical circuit construction, oscilloscope measurement, waveform analysis, Multisim computer-aided circuit simulation.
* **Contents:**
  * `experiments/` — Laboratory experiment documentation and reports.
  * `lab 5/` — Specific lab assignment work.
  * `multisim/` — NI Multisim simulation schema files.

### 🔹 [CSE 241 — Object-Oriented Programming](./CSE241-Object-Oriented-Programming/)
* **Key Topics:** C++ Classes & Objects, Constructors/Destructors, Pointers & Memory Management, Function/Operator Overloading, Inheritance, Polymorphism, Templates.
* **Contents:**
  * `CSE 241 SLIDES/` — Full semester presentation slides covering C++ OOP.
  * `quizs/` — Class quizzes and solutions.
  * *Absolute C++ (6th Edition)* by Walter Savitch.
